Bertrand de Coatpont (Pedro's manager, Sr Director) **likes the concrete, not just the concepts.** Confirmed 2026-06-08: he liked the concrete harness-varieties slide, but said he couldn't concretize the two abstract propositions (there-waiting + consistency layer), and when offered three angles (experience / process / artifacts) he picked **"les artefacts"** — the real deliverables.

**Why:** Bertrand is the manager who carries Pedro's promotion case upward, and he is requirements-first. An abstract proposition does not move him; a concrete scene + named artifacts + a working mechanism does. Reading him as concept-first leads to comms that don't land with the one person whose read matters most for the Director→Senior Director move. This is both a promotion lever and a daily-comms rule.

**How to apply:** in anything Pedro sends Bertrand — deck, Slack, 1:1 prep, proposal, status — **lead with the concrete**: a user scene or a before/after, the named artifacts that already exist (pages, schemas, a test — receipts, not concepts), a real number. Push the abstract framing and the mechanism to the *end*, after the concrete has landed. Show that the thing exists ("not concepts — pages, a schema, and a test, already in motion"), don't just describe what it would be. When a proposition isn't landing with him, don't explain more — drop a scene and show the receipts. Offer choices scoped ("experience / process / artifacts — which is fuzzy?"), not blank.
