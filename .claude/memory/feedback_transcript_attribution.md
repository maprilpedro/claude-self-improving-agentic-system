When reading Teams meeting transcripts, room-based microphone entries (e.g., "CR SJ ET15/Kettering VC (9)", "CR BASL 05/RIFFELSEE VC (5)") are not people — they are conference room devices.

**Why:** Pedro corrected this after I attributed all "CR" lines to an unknown person. In the March 23, 2026 AEM PM Virtual Working Session I, all "CR" lines were Loni Stark (VP PM AEM), who was presenting from a conference room.

**How to apply:** When a transcript shows a room-style label (contains "VC", "ET", room numbers, location names), infer from context who was in that room and attribute accordingly. Do not treat the room label as a person's name or initials.

**Important — room mics map per-meeting, not per-person.** A given conference room device label (e.g., "CR BUCH") will reflect whoever is occupying that room on a given day. Do not carry forward an attribution from one meeting to the next. Re-attribute fresh each transcript using context cues (named addressing, voice content, role-fit, meeting invite).
