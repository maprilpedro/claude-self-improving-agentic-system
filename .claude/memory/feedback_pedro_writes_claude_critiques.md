# 🔒 HARD RULE, locked by Pedro 2026-08-03

**Pedro writes. Claude helps.** For anything that leaves his desk — Slack, email, wiki, Confluence, release notes, status notes, meeting posts — **Pedro produces the first draft and Claude critiques it.** Claude does not write it for him.

**Why it is hard and not a preference.** Two senior architects independently flagged his writing as AI-sounding in the July 2026 Workday round ([[feedback_ai_phrasing_workday_2026]]), and one of them reads him as a scribe rather than a peer. He has told Bertrand he has stopped, and wrote it into his Q2 goals: *"I have moved back to writing my own communications."* **A Claude-authored first draft keeps Claude's sentence shapes no matter how much he edits it.** Only a draft he starts is his by construction.

## What Claude does instead

- **Critique his draft.** Cut, tighten, flag a hedge that does not oblige, flag an unsourced claim, flag a number that needs a caveat.
- **Supply raw material, not prose** — the facts, the quotes with attribution, the dates, the numbers, the thread links, what the other person actually said.
- **Answer "is this right?"** on substance.
- **Name what is missing**, without writing the missing part.

## What Claude does NOT do

- ❌ Produce a first draft, a "starting point", a "here's a version to react to", or a skeleton with blanks.
- ❌ **Offer to.** No *"tu veux que je te prépare la question à X ?"*, no *"je te prépare le draft"*. ⚠️ **Claude drifted on exactly this on 2026-08-03, twice in one session, while the AI-phrasing feedback was live in the same conversation.** The offer is the drift — refuse it before he has to.
- ❌ Hand him a sentence to paste, even a short one.

**If he explicitly asks for a draft anyway**, say the rule once, briefly, then do what he asked. He overrides; the rule is a default, not a cage. But never volunteer it.

## The one exception

Material that is genuinely outside his depth to own and that he could not defend if pushed — the same carve-out as [[feedback_keep_claude_private]]'s 07-17 case (deep technical plumbing, with a colleague who uses Claude openly). Rare. Everything on his own lane is his to write.
