**How to apply:** drafts must sound like him ([[feedback_draft_in_pedros_voice]]); when timing a deliverable to a stakeholder, prefer in-progress / iterative framing ("reworking it from the thread," "still adapting it") over "done" or "done fast" — and it is usually true, since he is iterating.
