Pedro keeps Claude "sous le coude" (in his back pocket), a private tool. Never frame, reveal, or suggest crediting output as AI-assisted to colleagues. And pace or frame deliverables so they do not signal superhuman speed.

**Why:** revealing AI assistance discounts his work and erodes his edge; a polished artifact produced overnight reads as "the AI wrote it" and raises the same flag. Surfacing him as someone who leans on an assistant cuts against the Senior Director positioning.

**How to apply:** drafts must sound like him ([[feedback_draft_in_pedros_voice]]); when timing a deliverable to a stakeholder, prefer in-progress / iterative framing ("reworking it from the thread," "still adapting it") over "done" or "done fast" — and it is usually true, since he is iterating. Never propose that he mention or thank an assistant. Seen 2026-05-28 (the Ian blog: he did not want to show he could produce a polished blog overnight).

**⚠️ The rule is default, not absolute — it protects Pedro's positioning, so where surfacing Claude does NOT cost him positioning, it does not apply. Do not flag those as violations (corrected 2026-07-17).** Two conditions that make surfacing fine, and they held together on 2026-07-17 (Pedro DM'd Felix Delval the raw client_id/OBO check attributed to "mon Claude"):
- **The colleague already uses Claude openly.** With someone who runs it themselves (Felix Delval does), naming it is peer-normal, not an admission of leaning on a crutch. It costs no edge because they share the tool.
- **The content is genuinely outside Pedro's depth to own.** The client_id/OBO plumbing is too technical for him to pass off as his own analysis, and pretending otherwise would be the bigger exposure (he could not defend it if pushed). Attributing it is honest AND safe — Felix reads it as "Pedro's assistant surfaced a good question", not "Pedro doesn't understand his own lane", precisely because Felix knows the tool's register.
Pedro's own words: *"Felix utilise ouvertement Claude, et ce que tu as capturé est trop technique pour moi, c'est pour cela que j'ai dévoilé ton intervention."* So the test before flagging is not "did he surface Claude" but **"does surfacing it here cost him positioning?"** — with a Claude-using peer, on content he cannot credibly own solo, the answer is no. **Still applies fully** with non-Claude colleagues, on his own lane, and to superhuman-speed polished artifacts (the 2026-05-28 Ian blog).
