**When Pedro iterates an outward draft with Claude, the job is fact-drift detection first, prose second.** Across ~10 rounds on the 2026-08-11 Loni note, every genuinely valuable catch was a **contradiction against something he had already written to someone else**, not a sentence improvement.

What was actually caught, and none of it was about writing quality:
- **Three different panel dates** in circulation (09-11 in the note, ~09-21 in the canvas, *"milieu-fin novembre"* to Bertrand twelve hours earlier) — and at one point **two of them inside the same message, six lines apart**.
- **"These are four things I am watching"** followed by five items.
- **A link to the Slack channel instead of the canvas**, which would have dropped a VP into a 200-message feed.
- **"Bertrand has reviewed this"** written before Bertrand had reviewed it, in a message Bertrand was cc'd on. A sentence that becomes false on send.
- Bug bash **19** vs the **17-21** he gave Bertrand; activation **26** vs the **25** he gave Bertrand that morning.

**Why:** Pedro runs many threads in parallel and restates the same program to different people hours apart. His prose is fine. His exposure is that the same fact arrives at the same reader twice with different values, which is what makes a plan look unstable. **Read the draft against his own recent Slack, not against a style guide.**

**How to apply:** before commenting on any sentence, diff every date, count and named commitment in the draft against what he has told the same recipients in the last 48h. Lead the review with the divergences. Put prose notes last, and cap them.

---

**Second, smaller rule — the drafting register, if he ever lifts [[feedback_pedro_writes_claude_critiques]].**

He did lift it once on 2026-08-11, explicitly (*"Override cette fois"*), then rejected the result as **"trop machine by the book"**. What failed was a seven-block labelled template. What worked was, in order:

1. A **skeleton with his own existing content already sorted into it**, holes marked — this is what unblocked his understanding, not prose.
2. Then **extending from the opening he had written himself**, in his register (no headers, short declaratives, plain words).
3. Then **span-level edits only** ([[feedback_edit_the_span_not_the_artifact]]).

An override is for one artifact. It does not weaken the standing rule.
