When the user reports overwhelm on a Status file with 10+ items marked 🔴, the problem is usually calibration, not throughput. Subtract before adding.

**Why:** April 24, 2026 session. Pedro had ~25 live threads with 12 items tagged 🔴 on EH Status file. His words: "I think I am in an ok state. But it's super overwhelming. Help me out." The diagnostic move was to audit how many items were actually load-bearing this week (four) and demote the rest under a "parked" label. Pedro confirmed this explicitly: "I agree with the 5 top priorities — how are they reflected in my projects?" The overwhelm lifted as soon as the list got recalibrated. It was never a capacity issue.

**How to apply:**
- When Pedro says overwhelmed, do not add. Audit the existing list first.
- Flag if 🔴 count exceeds 4-5. Some are miscoded and need demotion.
- Name the load-bearing items (tied to the next milestone or deadline). Everything else gets "parked past [date]" as an explicit visual demotion.
- Frame it as "Senior Directors carry 3-5 top-of-mind items, not 25" — this is the level Pedro is aiming for, and recalibration IS the Senior Director move.
- Do NOT respond to overwhelm by producing another framework or plan. The action is subtraction, not addition. "Permission granted to drop things" is often the unlock.
