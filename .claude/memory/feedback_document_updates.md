name: Brief summary after document updates
description: When updating documents, always give a brief resume of what changed and why
type: feedback
---

When updating any document (Obsidian notes, State of the Project, Stakeholder Map, Questions for Sorin, memory files, etc.), always follow up with a brief summary of what was changed and why.

**Why:** User asked for this explicitly. Without it, they have no quick way to know what got added or modified without re-reading the whole doc.

**How to apply:** After every edit to any document, include a short bullet list or sentence stating which doc was changed, what was added/removed/updated, and the reason (what new info prompted the change).
