**Source: Workday "Feedback Received", 7 reviewers, 2026-07-03 → 2026-07-31.** Pedro shared it 2026-08-03. He has already raised it with Bertrand, said he was aware and would stop, and is waiting on Bertrand's mid-year review.

## The two independent AI-phrasing flags (n=2, 15 days apart, different orgs)

- **Ian Boston, 2026-07-14:** *"At times communication style is a little bit too much like AI **even when not being AI generated**, and so for some audiences there is a risk that **the communication is not seen as his own, and discarded by those that habitually ignore AI generated output**. I think this would be a pity although we may all be subject to language modification by our exposure to too much AI."*
- **Felix Meschberger, 2026-07-29:** *"Documents and Slack comments from Pedro often smell like 'AI phrasing'. **I am not sure, whether these documents and comments are really AI-generated or not.** But to me it feels like **artificial and not genuine**."*

**Neither of them asserted that the output is AI-generated.** Ian explicitly says "even when not being AI generated" and offers cover ("we may all be subject to language modification"). Felix says "I am not sure". The confirmed fact is a **register** problem, not a disclosure event. ⚠️ **Do not draft anything that concedes more than the record does**, and do not re-open the Bertrand conversation to re-litigate it ([[feedback_dont_litigate_prior_replies]]) — forward-framed only from here.

## 🔴 The harder finding, and it is not the AI line

Felix Meschberger, same round, "Anything else you think I should know?":

> *"Pedro is a nice person to talk to and discuss. However, **I am sometimes not sure, whether he understands, what I am talking about. I am also not sure, if he really understands the topic that he is working on.** This may be a false impression, for sure. I have said Pedro is dependable, but **a good scribe is also dependable.**"*

And his "doing well" answer is the same sentence from the other side: *"Pedro is very dependable and conscientious in **taking notes and recording points of discussion**. I can really go back to the notes."*

**His compliment IS the criticism.** The AI-phrasing observation is his *evidence*, not his complaint: polished output that never shows a judgment reads as transcription. **So "I will stop using AI phrasing" under-treats it.** Plainer prose that still summarizes rather than decides leaves Meschberger's read exactly where it is. The only thing that moves it is a visible call — a position, a disagreement held, a decision defended in front of him.

**🔑 This is external, unprompted validation of [[Say the Sentence That Obliges — the Hedge Transfers the Ask]]** (promoted the same day from the killed H-007, on internal evidence only). A scribe obliges nobody. Meschberger describes from outside the exact failure mode the System Review isolated from inside. Two independent instruments, same reading.

## How to apply — this changes how drafting works

1. **The voice rules are now a defence, not a preference.** [[feedback_draft_in_pedros_voice]] and [[feedback_plain_language_no_jargon]] were quality bars. They are now the countermeasure to a documented perception held by two senior architects. Enforce them harder than before, and on Slack comments too, not only long artifacts — Meschberger named "documents **and Slack comments**".
2. **Bias to Pedro writing the first draft, Claude criticizing it.** The tell is register uniformity. A draft he writes and I cut is his voice by construction; a draft I write and he edits keeps my sentence shapes. On anything Meschberger, Ian Boston, or a wide channel will read, prefer critic mode.
3. **Every outward artifact needs one visible judgment.** Not a summary of positions, not options laid out neutrally. One sentence where Pedro says what should happen and why, which he would defend if challenged. That is the anti-scribe move and it is the same rule as the ownership sentence.
4. **Ban the tells that read as artificial** even when he wrote them: symmetrical three-part lists, "it's worth noting", "that said", recap-of-the-other-person openings, headers on short messages, and any sentence narrating the move instead of making it.
5. [[feedback_keep_claude_private]] still holds and is now evidenced. ⚠️ Its 07-17 exception (Felix **Delval** uses Claude openly) is unaffected — Delval's feedback in this same round is warm and has no AI remark. **Do not generalize the exception to Meschberger.**
