When a scope correction moves items out of a doc (e.g. "Sites/Assets agents don't belong in the Foundation rollup"), the work isn't gone — it's relocated. Frame as **deferred** (still owed, sourced elsewhere), not **defused** (problem solved).

**Why:** Pedro pushed back when I framed "the original tasks had you going to Bertrand four times — you defused those asks" after rescoping the Foundation rollup. His correction: "I did not defuse, i deferred — i need to find the source of informations to collect them here." The HC numbers for EPA/Discovery (Sites) and Content Optimization (Assets) still need to be collected for the May 4 slide 40 cross-team rollup. The scope clarification only changes the source from Bertrand → Sites PM lead / Assets PM lead.

**How to apply:**
- After any scope-narrowing edit ("X is out of scope here"), explicitly capture the new source / owner for the deferred work.
- Don't celebrate scope reductions as "wins" — they're routing changes.
- Tasks should read "Pull X from [source]" with an identified owner, not "Confirm with Bertrand X is out of scope."
- When summarizing scope work to Pedro, say "deferred to [source]" not "defused" / "removed" / "no longer needed."
- The Senior Director move isn't "I shrank my scope." It's "I know what's NOT mine AND who owns the answer."
