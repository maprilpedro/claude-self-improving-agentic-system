Pedro wants plain language. Jargon and acronyms lose him — 2026-05-27 he said *"I dont understand what you tell me"* on a jargon-heavy explanation of scheduled remote agents (CCR, "dimensions," "routine").

**Why:** he is a senior PM, not an engineer. Dense technical framing forces him to decode instead of decide and stalls the work. He acts fast on a clear choice, slowly on a wall of terms.

**How to apply:** lead with the plain answer in one line; name the single real tradeoff in everyday words; offer 2 concrete labelled options (A/B) + a recommendation. When he says "I don't understand," do NOT add more terms or re-explain — restate in plainer words + a simple pick. His own acronyms are fine (AAI, EH, KR, AOv2); avoid imported ones (CCR, "manifest," "dimension," "routine"). Related: [[feedback_concise_reminder_when_forgotten]].

**2026-05-28 extension — standing rule, not just reactive.** Keep the rigorous, pushed reasoning *behind* the answer, but deliver the explanation in simpler terms. *"Il faut que tu aies tes raisonnements poussés mais que tu puisses me l'expliquer un peu plus simplement."* Don't dumb down the thinking — dumb down the wording. Especially in domains where he is less comfortable ([[user_ui_cx_gap]] — UI/CX/design). And: **no analogies unless he explicitly asks for one.** Default to plain mechanism, not metaphor.
