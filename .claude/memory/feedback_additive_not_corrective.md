When you build on a senior person's artifact (a diagram, a doc, an architecture), present your additions as **additive** ("the two layers I'd add"), never as **corrective** ("what's MISSING"). Same content, opposite signal.

**Why:** marking up the master's work in red reads as "you missed things" = belittling the owner (`never outshine the master`), and risks turning a co-author into a defensive party. Additive framing keeps them an amplifier. Concrete case: the Ian NorthStar diagram (2026-05-28) — the internal working version stamped "MISSING" in solid red on Ian's boxes; the version to share was reframed to red-outline additions, "Memory MCP · shared (Ian)" credit, and a line "grey is yours, red-outlined is what I'd add."

**How to apply:** red-outline (not red-fill) for your additions; credit their parts by name; send the words/question before the diagram; lead with "what I'd add" and "does this match how you see it?" Pairs with [[feedback_dont_litigate_prior_replies]] and the leadership pattern "Co-Author With the Owner." Seen across Ian rounds 1-3 (2026-05-27 to 28).
