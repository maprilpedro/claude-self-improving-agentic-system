## Why this matters to Pedro (three things)

**1. It gives him AEP's own vocabulary for the coexistence argument.** Engagement **Type B = "Customer Co-Innovation AI Pod — time-boxed co-innovation sprint (defined at kickoff call in writing). AI-native, agentic focus."** A POD customer is, by the program's own definition, **co-innovating, not migrating**. That is exactly the distinction Pedro has been defending as activation ≠ migration ([[reference_coworker_enablement]]) — except here AEP wrote it. **The kill-switch ask to Rachel Hanessian stops being an exception request and becomes "treat these orgs as what your own program calls them."**

**2. 🔑 THE NAMED GENERALIZATION OWNER IS UNCLAIMED FOR AEM.** The program requires, at qualification: a *"Generalizable Hypothesis — **Named PM-owned thesis** for how work becomes platform capability."* At kickoff: a **"Named Generalization Owner — PM from the relevant AEP/App area. Accountable for graduating co-built capabilities into product."** And at the **Phase 05 Contribution Review Gate**, gate **G2 Platform Fit** asks literally *"Is there a defined home in AEP or an App product for this capability? **Has a product owner accepted ownership?**"*
> **AMEX is in build. Coca-Cola is coming. Both produce artifacts that land in AEM** (content fragments → EPA manifest; Discovery → central manifest). **Nobody is named.** Same figure as the parity list — **claim it, do not request it** ([[The Game Itself — Position Over Merit]], [[Govern a Consistency Layer Over Primitives You Don't Own]]). Without a named owner the contribution fails G2 and AEM inherits a fork it did not choose.

**3. "No Snowflakes" is Pedro's manifest position, written by AEP.** *"Pod work is not a license to fork the product for one account."* AVOID one-off forks, hard-coded customer logic. INSTEAD *"build through supported extension points — Skills, MCP integrations, Workflows, and configuration"*, *"design for reuse"*, ***"contribute it back — pod deliverables are candidates for the core product and roadmap."*** Architecture/UI exceptions **need ARB approval**. → Backs Felix's *"have the different teams contribute their marketplace"* and Pedro's curation lane. **Citable against any AMEX-specific build.**

## Mechanics

- **Scope types:** CX Coworker · **CX Coworker Skills** · CX Coworker MCPs/Connectors. **AEM's whole lane is "Skills".**
- **Qualification (all four):** Strategic Account approved · Real Problem at Scale · **Generalizable Hypothesis (named PM)** · Customer Co-Investment (named technical PoC + protected capacity).
- **Intake:** submit intake → essentials (customer, tier, scope, goal, sponsor, pod lead) → **legal clearance** → approval/denial. Tracked in a "pod record"; a Summary tab gives an AI-generated portfolio rollup.
- **🔴 LEGAL GATE, required BEFORE the first customer meeting: the "AI Participation Terms for AI Pods."** Five fields (Account name · **ECCID** · address · signatory name · signatory email), emailed to **`ogcaiprogramcontracts@adobe.com`**. Deck: *"NOTE: this should change in early August."* ⚠️ **Whether this is the same object as the "GenAI rider" Apoorva asked about is UNVERIFIED — do not conflate** ([[feedback_dont_conflate_pattern_with_object]]).
- **Contribution Review Gate (Phase 05), four criteria:** G1 Generalizability (*"applicable to ≥2 other strategic accounts"*) · **G2 Platform Fit (a product owner must accept ownership)** · G3 Engineering Standards · G4 AI Fluency Alignment. Any fail → Disposition Review.
- Duration ~5 months, bi-weekly deployments, **daily Coworker exec standups**, quarterly closed-loop review (*"triggers restructure if loop fails two cycles"*).
- Escalation routing: scope creep → Sponsor + PM · enhancement requests → **PM (roadmap fit)** · product/security/data risk → Sponsor immediately · commercial → Account Team.
- Bugs route through **standard Adobe support, "not through FDE as a side-door."**
