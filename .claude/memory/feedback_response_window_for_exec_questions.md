When Bertrand (or any exec) asks Pedro for input ahead of a meeting that's happening **tonight / in a few hours**, the response window is short — typically 30 minutes max, often less. Pedro needs the answer ready when he sees the question, not 90 minutes later.

**Why:** April 28, 2026 — Bertrand asked Pedro "Agentic AI → where do we see user adoption? Hypothesis? Adjustments: Team" ahead of Loni's staff that evening. I gave Pedro a deep multi-part analysis (data tiers, screenshot interpretation, strategic positioning) when what was needed was a 5-sentence adjustments-forward Slack reply Pedro could fire immediately. By the time the analysis was tightened into a deliverable, Bertrand had likely walked into the meeting without it. Pedro flagged: "well he probably walked into the meeting without my proposals."

**How to apply:**
- When Pedro shares an exec question with a near-term meeting deadline, **lead with the 5-sentence answer first**. Backup brief and screenshots come AFTER.
- Match the question's frame literally. "Adjustments: Team" = forward actions with named owners. Not data-quality reframes, not strategic-positioning meta-commentary.
- Don't volunteer doubts about Pedro's own data unprompted. Senior Directors don't undermine their own platform in front of the boss; they show pressure-testing AFTER being asked.
- If a deeper analysis is genuinely useful, label the second window: "send this now; the longer analysis is for your 1:1 with Bertrand or for the May 4 prep."
- Keep a 5-sentence answer pre-stageable for the obvious recurring questions: "where's adoption", "what's the next product gap", "what would you cut", "what's the team adjustment", "what's the compliance read."

**The cost of getting this wrong:** Pedro's reporting platform is the only credible source for agent adoption answers. When Bertrand asks and Pedro doesn't respond in time, Bertrand has to improvise in front of Loni, and Pedro's platform doesn't get its name in the room. That's a missed visibility moment for Senior Director track.
