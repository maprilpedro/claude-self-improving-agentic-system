When Pedro asks "what was the plan again?" or "remind me about X" — give the punchline + key actions in 5 sentences max. Not a full restatement.

**Why:** May 1, 2026 session — Pedro asked *"SD-1 - what is the question or plan again?"* after a long day. He had context fatigue, not amnesia. The right reminder leads with the move (the Senior Director claim) and the exit line (the durable sentence to use afterward), then the 2-3 questions to ask in the meeting. Five sentences. Not the original 30-line task description from KR3.

**How to apply:**

- Lead with the *move*: who Pedro is meeting, what he's claiming, what he's leaving with.
- Name the *exit line* — the durable sentence Pedro can drop in subsequent conversations.
- Cap at 5 sentences. The full prep is in the KR note; he can re-read if he wants.
- If the original plan has shifted since it was drafted (e.g., the meeting moved, a new stakeholder surfaced), name the shift in the same response. Don't restate the original verbatim if it's now stale.
- Pair the reminder with a "no urgency dropped — just deliberate now" framing if a deadline moved. Reduces the pressure of perceived slippage.

**Example:** SD-1 reminder, May 1, 2026 — *"Haresh Kumar, Sr Director PMM AEM. The move: claim the PMM/monetization framing for the agent portfolio before someone else does. Walk in, ask three questions in order — who owns PMM, what monetization model, are there directional revenue projections per agent. Leave with the durable line 'I'm working with Haresh on the monetization framing for the agent portfolio.' With the meeting moved, urgency drops but value rises."*
