# The Theses Held — Push the Function, Not the Abstraction; Hold the Surface Loose

_Section: Distributed-Harness Architecture (AOv2 / Agentic NorthStar) — part of `ai-product/`; router = README.md._
- **Date identified**: 2026-06-19 (stock-take on "do consistency-layer + there-waiting still work, or am I digging in?")
- **Source**: Independent validations in the 72h window 2026-06-17→19 — the AEM↔Coworker working session (2026-06-18, Manas Garg, transcript `AEM Coworker Sync/20260618`), Bertrand de Coatpont's public Slack validation (2026-06-18), Felix Meschberger + Ian Reasor in the P42 thread (2026-06-18). Weighed against the twice-observed "couldn't concretize" failure (Silvia 2026-06-05, Bertrand earlier).
- **Insight**: Both theses are **alive and being arrived at independently by others**, which is the signal that separates a held position from stubbornness. (a) **Consistency layer** — Bertrand, public: *"the concept of decoupling the harness from the chat experience is getting traction"*; Felix M: companion mode *"is a UI problem not a backend/harness problem… independent of the harness"*; Ian Reasor: the API is the consumer-agnostic durable substrate, the harness is one consumer. (b) **There-waiting** — Manas, the platform owner himself: side-by-side/companion is *"the bread and butter of AEM,"* and the companion-mode thread (Bertrand's two gaps, Natalia Venditto's EW PoC) makes embedded presence a live platform question, not Pedro's solo idea. **But validation of the thesis is not license to push it the way that already failed twice.** Three disciplines fall out: **(1) Push the function, not the abstraction.** The abstract pitch ("coherence above the harness") does not travel cold — proven twice. The vehicles that move it are concrete artifacts in a pull context: the renderer registry, the door-maps, the Josh rendering session (the consistency layer *demonstrated* as "each team publishes its own renderer against a shared contract"), not a reposted abstraction. **(2) Hold the surface loose — own the function/contract, not the real estate.** "EH = the consistency layer" as a *surface claim* has a contested flank (the Basel Flywheel positioned EW as the full surface — "the train left the station"; the PLG/standalone-EW question is open). The consistency layer as a *PM-owned function above the harnesses* is solid. Engage capital on *"here is the cross-surface contract the system must honour,"* not on *"EH the surface wins"* — same split as Ian owns the memory service / Pedro owns the requirement. **(3) Keep the abstraction out of hot eng threads** (P42, where Michael Marth won't approve); footprint there = reporting only, the map↔P42 bridge is carried by Felix/Reasor, not Pedro.
- **Why it matters**: this is the answer to "am I being stubborn?" — **no, when the thesis is independently validated; yes, if I keep pushing it through the channel that failed.** The fix is never to abandon a validated thesis; it is to change the vehicle (concrete + pull, not abstract + push) and to refuse to marry the function to a surface whose fate is unresolved. This is [[position-over-merit]] applied to ideas: the position is held by *being the foundation/contract*, not by *defending a surface box* (same lesson as the Aditi position-win — own the layer use-cases build on, don't wall the territory).
- **Corroboration (2026-06-19) — a second harness provably exists, which hardens the substrate-is-the-hedge half**: in the Coworker topics thread, Ian Boston disclosed that **AIP/AUP are building their own harness, "intended for all Adobe the same way Ethos is for all Adobe," already used by Photoshop, Firefly and others**, on an independent path from AEP's Coworker (Dan Foygel: contact for a while but *"taken independent paths so far… convergence… hasn't been a business priority"*). Plus Ian's own framing: a harness = an Agent SDK (Claude, Codex), *"wrap and adapt, don't implement one."* So there are now **provably multiple harnesses** (Coworker/AEP, the AIP all-Adobe harness, Claude Code/Codex SDKs) on non-converging paths. This is the strongest form of the two-way-door argument: the harness layer is not just *swappable in theory*, it is *already plural and diverging in practice* — which makes the portable skills + API substrate above the harnesses the only durable layer **and** the hedge against AEM betting on the wrong harness. The thesis is not "pick the right harness"; it is "own the layer that survives whichever harness wins." **Sharpened 2026-06-19 (Ian Boston, on a forced-Coworker DACI):** a platform/harness standard cannot be *mandated* into existence — *"a DACI requiring all teams to move to CoWorker will not succeed and will damage architectural alignment… we need guidance that is followed more than pure and perfect guidance that no one follows."* The only measure that matters is **willing adoption + delivered quality**, not a mandate. So the durable PM play is doubly confirmed: you don't win by getting a harness blessed as The Standard (it can be ignored); you win by owning the portable layer (skills/API/definition) that every harness has to consume regardless of which one teams adopt.
