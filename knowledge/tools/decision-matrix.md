# PM Tools & Methods Decision Matrix

> When to use which PM tool, method, or framework. Based on evidence, not preference.

## Research Methods

| Method | Best For | Avoid When | Sample Size | Cost | Speed |
|--------|----------|------------|-------------|------|-------|
| User Interviews | Deep "why" understanding | You need statistical significance | 5-15 | Medium | Slow |
| Surveys | Quantifying known issues | Exploring unknown problems | 100+ | Low | Fast |
| Analytics/Data | Behavioral patterns | Understanding motivation | N/A | Low | Fast |
| Usability Testing | Interaction problems | Strategic questions | 5-8 | Medium | Medium |
| A/B Testing | Optimizing known solutions | Discovering new solutions | 1000+ | Low | Medium |
| Diary Studies | Long-term behavior | Quick tactical decisions | 10-20 | High | Slow |
| Card Sorting | Information architecture | Visual design | 15-30 | Low | Medium |
| Contextual Inquiry | Workflow understanding | Remote users | 5-10 | High | Slow |

## Communication Tools

| Situation | Tool/Approach | Why |
|-----------|--------------|-----|
| Exec alignment | One-pager with decision needed | Execs scan, don't read |
| Team alignment | Working session, not presentation | Participation builds buy-in |
| Saying no | "Not now" with criteria for yes | Preserves relationship |
| Bad news | Data first, then interpretation | Removes defensiveness |
| Cross-team dependency | Written agreement with milestones | Memory fades, docs don't |
