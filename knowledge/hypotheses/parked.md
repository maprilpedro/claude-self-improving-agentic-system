1. **Expected 2nd instance** — name *where* the next independent instance would plausibly show up (a forum, a person, an artifact type). "Somewhere eventually" = don't park; keep it as a line in the session memory block instead.

Promote at a clean **2nd independent instance** (distinct events, not two reps of one event). Verdicts are recorded here; promoted/dropped/folded rows move to History below — never deleted.
