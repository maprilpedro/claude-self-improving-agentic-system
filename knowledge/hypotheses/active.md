## H-008: "Razor-and-Blades" Rhetoric Is a Decline Tell, Not a Strategy

- **Status**: Proposed — **n=1, low confidence** (Pedro raised it; not yet a pattern)
- **Date proposed**: 2026-06-25
- **Category**: Market signals / org behavior (PM-leadership lens)
- **Source signal**: Pedro, 2026-06-25 (Slack musing to Bertrand): *"Une de mes boîtes a commencé à utiliser l'analogie des lames de rasoir au début de sa chute."* = a former employer started invoking the razor-and-blades model (give the tool, monetize the recurring consumable) right as the company began declining.
- **Hypothesis**: The razor-and-blades *model* is healthy (Gillette, printers, consoles). The **decline tell** is *when* a team starts reciting it: an org reaches for "give the value away now, monetize the rest later" most loudly when the paid product has lost standalone traction — the rhetoric substitutes for traction. So razor-and-blades framing appearing *as a justification* (not a pre-planned model) predicts decline.
- **Test design**: watch for a 2nd independent instance (another company/team invoking razor-blades rhetoric while slipping). Adobe live tie-in: the PLG / TBYB / $0-SKU push (Felix's PLG paper, Brand Concierge self-service trial). The discriminant test — is the agent given free *because confident paid usage follows* (healthy) or *because paid usage isn't decoupling* (the tell)? Corey's EPA signal (*"people try it, they fail, they leave"*) is the watch-this-space case.
- **Evidence for**: 1 anecdote (Pedro's former employer). Coheres with FB-031 (position-over-merit) loosely — rhetoric-as-substitute-for-substance.
- **Evidence against**: n=1; survivorship/recency bias (you remember the analogy because the company later fell); razor-blades succeeds far more often than it signals decline. **Do not promote without a 2nd clean observation.**
- **If confirmed**: a cheap leadership early-warning heuristic + a lens to pressure-test Adobe's own PLG framing (ask the discriminant question in the strat-plan).
- **If killed**: drop — it was pattern-matching on a single memorable failure.
- **2026-08-03 System Review — KEPT ONE CYCLE, HARD AUTO-KILL 2026-09-01.** Still n=1 after 5.5 weeks; zero second observation; the named Adobe discriminant test (is the agent given free *because confident paid usage follows*, or *because paid usage isn't decoupling*?) was never asked of the live PLG / TBYB / $0-SKU case. This is the same state that got two parked candidates dropped at 07-01. **It survives this review only because the discriminant question is cheap and genuinely useful to ask in the strat-plan — so ask it.** If 2026-09-01 arrives with no 2nd observation and no discriminant answer, kill it as untested-and-deprioritized (the H-001/H-002 precedent) without further discussion.
