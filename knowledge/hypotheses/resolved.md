## H-005: Owning Cross-Agent Measurement Standardization Creates Structural Cross-Org Influence for the Experience Hub PM

- **Status**: Confirmed
- **Date proposed**: 2026-03-26
- **Date resolved**: 2026-05-03
- **Category**: Leadership / Strategy
- **Original hypothesis**: "We believed that the Experience Hub PM owning the cross-agent measurement standard — by driving adoption of a shared TSR/VRR baseline across all agent teams — would create durable cross-org influence, because measurement standards become infrastructure that every team depends on. The owner of the standard gets visibility into every agent's performance and a seat at every agent roadmap conversation."
- **Resolution**: Confirmed at the spirit-of-hypothesis level. The mechanism worked — owning the data substrate produced positional authority Pedro didn't have on March 26. The original test (count agent teams that adopt a literal shared TSR/VRR standard) was overtaken by a more meaningful set of confirmations: public naming by the Sr Director, peer-team voluntary consolidation, named seat at every cross-agent forum, and an explicit three-tier architecture in which Pedro owns the middle tier.
- **Key evidence**:
  - **Public naming as AEM-AO liaison** (2026-04-14, AO 2.0 Strategy Meeting) — Bertrand answered "It's Pedro" when Jaclyn asked who owns the AEM-AO connection. In front of Conrad, Jaclyn, Yanira. Scope expansion beyond Experience Hub.
  - **Felix reports named at VP level** (2026-04-13, Loni H2 planning) — Bertrand named Pedro and the dashboard in Loni's H2 planning meeting. Public sponsorship at VP level.
  - **Loni's prompt-to-roadmap question Pedro now answers** (2026-04-13) — "Do we have a view of what percentage of the top requests and stuff are making it into the agent?" Bertrand: no. Pedro's JIRA pipeline + Priority Consolidation view answer it directly.
  - **Voluntary consolidation by peer team** (2026-04-22, Varun Kalra Discovery sync) — Varun voluntarily offered to retire his Discovery Agent wiki and consolidate into Pedro's platform. First peer-team consolidation. Quote: *"I want to streamline and finalize on how we can ensure that there's only one way we are creating the final reports."*
  - **Cross-org definition ownership in shared infrastructure** (2026-04-15-16, Rubin engagement) — Karthik (Rubin tech lead) accepted Pedro's 7-agent list as the AEM tagging definition in one exchange. Felix's Prod org list positioned as fix for AEP provisioning gap (AEM contributing to AEP infrastructure, not just consuming).
  - **Three-tier reporting architecture locked** (2026-05-01) — Pedro owns the middle tier (Portfolio Monthly Briefing, monthly, AEM-only narrative). QBR (PMM, quarterly) above; per-agent reports (weekly) below. Three artifacts, three audiences, one data source. Pedro named the architecture out loud — itself a Senior Director move.
  - **Portfolio Monthly Briefing v0 shipped** (2026-04-30) — first AEM-wide cross-agent intelligence artifact at the management-narrative tier. 4 of 6 sections substantive; cross-agent retention + reach in one place.
  - **Bertrand framing line that anchors the role** (2026-04-29, AO 2.0 strategy session): *"I would prefer us, AEM, to focus on the AEM use cases rather than building technology if there's a dedicated team for the infrastructure around it."* AEM owns use cases + practitioner experience + agent definitions; AO owns runtime. Pedro's seat is in the use-cases-and-definitions layer.
- **Lesson learned**: Cross-org influence accrues to the owner of the data substrate even more than to the owner of a literal "standard." The standard formalizes what the substrate already governs. Don't wait for a standard to ship before claiming the role — ship the substrate, name it out loud, and the standard becomes a downstream consequence. The original test design ("count teams that adopt a TSR/VRR standard") was a Director's test. The Senior Director's test is "are you in the room when the architecture is being decided?" Both true here.
- **Impact**: H-005 confirmation reframes Pedro's promotion case from *"I shipped a report"* to *"I own the AEM agent intelligence layer at the management-narrative tier and the AEM-AO connection at the strategy tier."* The new artifacts (Portfolio Monthly Briefing, AEM-AO liaison role, three-tier architecture statement) are the durable evidence Loni can point to in a Senior Director conversation. The Pedro narrative compounds: G1 (Agent Intelligence) is no longer aspirational, it's operational and externally validated.
- **Open derivative work** (not part of H-005, tracked elsewhere):
  - Capability-monthly usage as the headline value metric (BVR — see ai-product/) is the next-frontier extension of the same play
  - VRR 5-tier definition from Yanira's wiki — still owed for full literal-standard maturity, but no longer a gate on the influence outcome H-005 predicted
- **Superseded by**: None. H-005 is a leadership / scope hypothesis confirmed at the role level. Successor hypotheses on broadcast frequency (informal, May 1) and the management-tier artifact pattern (Portfolio Briefing as a repeatable template for other PMs) may emerge as formal H-007 / H-008.
