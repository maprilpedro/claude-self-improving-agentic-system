# Resolved Hypotheses

> Archive of confirmed and killed hypotheses. Never delete -- learn from both outcomes.

## Status Key
- **Confirmed**: Evidence supports the hypothesis
- **Killed**: Evidence contradicts the hypothesis
- **Superseded**: Replaced by a more refined hypothesis
