# Three-Storey Test — Conversation / Intelligence / Governance, Rent the Brain but Own the Learning

_Section: Strategic Patterns (external framework, applied) — part of `patterns/`; router = README.md._
- **Source**: YouTube channel *IA et Stratégie*, « Le modèle SaaS est brisé. Salesforce nous montre la fin du film » (vault note, 2026-09-02), on Claude Force (Benioff + Amodei, 2026-08-26).
- **Date**: 2026-09-02
- **The framework**: enterprise software splits into three storeys. **Conversation** (where work actually happens, Slack), **intelligence** (the model and the agent fleet), **governance** (data of record, permissions, 25 years of business logic). Each storey tries to commoditise the other two. Salesforce owns two (Slack + CRM), rents the third to Anthropic by default, and accepts to *"die as a counter to survive as a toll"*. The screen does not vanish, it becomes a **porthole**: agents work under the surface in APIs and surface only to be audited by a human.
- **The vendor test, three questions**: (1) do you own reference data the customer cannot rebuild elsewhere, (2) do you own the right to say who does what (permissions, compliance, the stamp), (3) do you **learn from each usage** in a way a replacement could not catch up. Three yes = a toll. Three no = a screen billed per seat, *"a lease in a building the owner is already evacuating"*. The rule in one line: **rent your intelligence, own your learning loop.** Same invisible position, two fates, banks vs Apple Pay and telcos vs WhatsApp became pipes, Intel Inside kept the margin, because Intel kept learning.
- **Dated exit signal (12 months)**: where do new Salesforce customers arrive from, via Claude/Slack/agents, or still via screens and sellers.

## Applied to Adobe / Coworker / AEM (2026-09-02)

| Storey | Salesforce | Adobe |
|---|---|---|
| Conversation | Slack, owned, with real usage | **Coworker**, built in-house, usage still being created (AEM customer GA 09-08, TBYB first 50). No owned messaging surface; M365/Teams is a bridge, not ownership |
| Intelligence | Claude, rented by default | Rented (model behind AO), plus the **semantic layer** (entity resolution, knowledge graph, cross-session memory) as Adobe's own learning loop ([[reference_coworker]]) |
| Governance | CRM data + permissions | **AEM**: content of record, DAM, ABAC, brand truth, approval lifecycle. Bertrand's 08-27 `#p42-architecture` post is this storey said in Adobe's words, approval state vs published as a first-class parameter, *"AEM the trusted context layer vs just another retrieval API"* |

- **Bertrand's four modes (08-25) map one to one**: Coworker standalone = conversation storey owned · AEM UI with Coworker panel = the screen as porthole · third-party client with Coworker headless = Headless 360 · third-party client with oneAEM MCP = the toll without the harness. Salesforce called itself a *"trusted enterprise harness"*, the word Adobe uses for Coworker.
- **Where AEM scores**: questions 1 and 2 are yes today. **Question 3 is the open one, and it is inside Adobe, not against Anthropic.** The conversations and traces land in Coworker/Rubin (AEP org). AEM's 18 GA plugins run in a manifest AEM does not own with no prompt surface (2026-09-01 finding). All 85 AEM skills carry `Usage Metrics: no` (2026-08-27 inventory). So AEM risks being the governance toll whose **learning loop is held by the harness team**, the internal version of the pipe. The counter is the lane Pedro already holds: the reporting definition, skill-level observability, the instruction layer ([[Definition Ownership Is the Moat on Shared Data Infrastructure]], [[Govern a Consistency Layer Over Primitives You Don't Own]]).
- **Seats**: the video's death is *"value living in a screen billed per seat"*. AEM is licensed per program, Coworker meters credits, so the seat cliff is not AEM's exposure. The exposure is the **$0 SKU population** (66 of 90 licensed at $0, Bertrand 08-24) read against the razor-and-blades tell in [[H-008]]. The video's own exit signal applies verbatim: **do AEM customers arrive via Coworker and agents, or via screens and sellers**, and Rubin can answer it if the definition is AEM's.
- **Experience Hub = the porthole**. The video's phrase is the EH thesis in one word: not a workplace, the place where a human audits what agents did (Security Health shipped through EH 07-30 is a porthole case). It also decides the suggested-prompts question by frame: prompts that start work belong in the conversation storey, EH shows outcomes.
- **The Cursor risk, internal form**: the supplier that becomes a rival does not cut AEM off, it makes it undifferentiated (AEM skills told they are an AEP coworker) or invisible (removed from `#aep-coworker-core` 07-28, manifests audited for removal 08-31).
- **When it fails**: the frame is a commentator's synthesis on one week-old announcement. *"By default"* is not exclusive, and Anthropic learns from the same conversations Salesforce encodes, so question 3 is unresolved on both sides even in the source's own reading. Do not use it to declare winners, use it to name which storey a decision sits on.
