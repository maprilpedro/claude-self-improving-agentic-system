# Platform Contribution Model with Quality Gate

_Section: Anti-Patterns — part of `patterns/`; router = README.md._
- **Date identified**: 2026-03-31
- **Observations**: (1) Experience Hub had 15+ teams requesting features from 2 engineers — unsustainable service desk dynamic. (2) Security was the only team that contributed back — proved self-service is possible. (3) EH Evolution proposal explicitly separates what EH gates (design, metric, lifecycle, testing) from what contributing teams own (build, data, monitoring).
- **Pattern**: Small platform teams cannot scale by building everything for consuming teams. The right model: define a contribution framework where consuming teams own their own features, and the platform team owns the quality bar. Platform team provides standards, component library, and approval gate. Consuming teams provide the build and the maintenance.
- **Three conditions for it to work**: (1) A published standard — teams need to know what "good" looks like before they build. (2) A gate with teeth — platform PM must be willing to reject contributions that don't meet the standard. (3) At least one proof point — one team that successfully contributed validates the model for others. Security in EH is that proof point.
- **🟢 Outcome validation, 2026-07-30 — the proof point shipped, four months on.** The entry's observation (2) was analysis-derived in March ("Security was the only team that contributed back — proved self-service is *possible*"). It is now **outcome-validated**: **Security Health is live in Experience Hub** — daily production scans surfacing under the Admin & IT profile, in a **new left-nav section the security team owns end to end ("Security and Compliance", which also lists Penetration Tests)**, with three OWASP-aligned finding types and CSV export. **EH supplied the surface, the profile targeting and the nav slot; the security team built, owns and operates the capability.** That is the contribution model working exactly as specified, not a pilot. Two things this proves that the March observation could not: (a) a contributing team will carry a capability all the way to production on someone else's surface, and (b) the contribution can claim **its own nav section**, not just a card — the slot granularity is bigger than the model assumed. ⚠️ **Still the same contributing team.** The model is validated, not yet generalized; the open question is whether a *second, different* team repeats it. Watch the assets team (Sylvia's DAM-metrics activation report, routed 2026-06-24 as "the owning team contributes it themselves, EH code-reviews") as the natural next case.
- **The narrative line this earns** (useful in any EH adoption or platform pitch): *the surface where a company exposes its operational posture is a platform, not a launchpad* — security today, more later. A shipped case, not a plan.
- **What the gate covers**: Design standards, conversion metric, time-to-live (TTL) commitment, test evidence.
- **When it fails**: When the platform PM says yes to everything to avoid friction. The surface becomes noise, users stop trusting it, and the original problem (too many asks, too few engineers) is worse than before.
