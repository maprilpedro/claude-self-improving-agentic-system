# Governance Posture Is a Senior Director Signal

_Section: Operating Discipline — part of `leadership/`; router = README.md._
- **Date identified**: 2026-03-27
- **Source**: Shankari 1:1 handoff, March 27.
- **Pattern**: Senior Directors hold a line that Directors often avoid holding because it creates friction. In a shared platform like Experience Hub, every team wants real estate. Saying no — or "show me the homework first" — is not popular. But it is the thing that keeps the product from becoming noise. Shankari's standard: no addition without a JIRA, design involvement, user research, and a hypothesis. No announcement without a conversion metric and a 4-week review date. No engineer going to engineering directly on Slack to "stick a button somewhere."
- **Why this is a leadership signal**: Directors tend to accommodate incoming requests to maintain relationships. Senior Directors enforce standards that protect the product's integrity even when it creates tension. The willingness to say "not without the homework" in a room with more senior people is a demonstration of conviction.
- **How to hold the line**: Be consistent, not arbitrary. When you push back, always cite the principle, not the person. "Here's what we require of every team that adds to Experience Hub" is harder to argue with than "I don't think this is ready."
