# Substitute-Hosting the Cross-Agent Forum Is Altitude, Not Cover

_Section: Operating at Senior Director Level — part of `leadership/`; router = README.md._

- **Date**: 2026-05-18 (logged 2026-05-19)
- **Source**: Pedro chaired the Agent Owners Alignment (the standing cross-agent forum, Yanira Castaneda's meeting) while Yanira was on PTO. He set the agenda (CCF update, AOv2 status, Philippe demo), delivered the AOv2 status himself, handed the floor to Bertrand for the roadmap-process update, and closed the room. Source: `Meeting Notes / Agent Owner Alignement / 20260518 - Agents Owners Alignement -`.
- **Insight**: Covering a peer's recurring meeting reads two ways. The Director read is *cover* — "I kept the lights on while Yanira was out." The Senior Director read is *altitude* — for one cycle Pedro was the person who owned the cross-agent venue, decided what the agent owners spent 30 minutes on, and was the one Bertrand handed the roadmap message to in front of every agent PM and engineer. The seat, not the words, is the signal. Standing in for the convener of a cross-team forum is a no-cost demonstration that you operate at the convener's altitude — leadership sees you running the room, not presenting into it.
- **Why it matters for the Director→Senior Director line**: the fuzzy part of that line is "operates cross-org vs operates a project." Owning the cross-agent forum, even as a substitute, is literal evidence of the former. It compounds with [[H-005]] (substrate-before-standard) — Pedro already owns the data substrate the room consumes; now he also, episodically, owns the room. Same axis: the forum and the substrate are the two things that make influence structural rather than positional.
- **How to convert it from one-off to pattern**: the value leaks if it stays a silent favor. The move is to make the backup-host role explicit and standing — offer Yanira (and signal to Bertrand) "I'll cover this whenever you're out." That converts an accident of timing into a named, repeatable role, and gives leadership a recurring reason to see Pedro in the convener seat. The offer is the entry `[[Pick Up the Open Action Item]]` applied to a forum instead of a task.
- **What it unlocked in the same session**: hosting is what made the [[Use the Forum to Manufacture the Deliverable and Pre-Load Its Consumers]] move executable — the host is the one who can put "produce the 1-pager" in front of the room and name the owner. Without the seat the forcing move is a Slack to Bertrand; with the seat it is a public, in-room commitment. The two entries are the same event read at two levels: the role (host) and the act the role enables (manufacture the deliverable).
- **Failure mode**: treating it as logistics ("I just ran Yanira's call"). Under-claiming a cross-org altitude signal is the same visibility gap Pedro has self-named. The discipline is to record it as evidence and make the role recur, not to wave it off as a favor.
- **Thinking lens**: *"You miss 100% of the shots you don't take."* The shot here is the standing-backup-host offer. Hosting once is luck; asking to be the default backup is the deliberate rep.
- **Status**: single instance (2026-05-18). Mechanism (own the convener seat, even as substitute, then make it recurring) is a recognized visibility technique; holding as a named entry, watch for the recurrence Pedro engineers via the standing-backup offer.
