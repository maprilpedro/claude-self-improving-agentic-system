# Separate the Strategic Position from the Capacity Conversation

_Section: Operating Discipline — part of `leadership/`; router = README.md._
- **Date identified**: 2026-03-26
- **Source**: Hero Surfaces one-pager. "Naming Experience Hub as the hero surface is a strategic position. What gets built on that foundation is a separate roadmap conversation."
- **Pattern**: One of the most common ways Director-level work gets blocked is by attaching strategic proposals to resource asks. Leadership can reject the resource ask and the strategy dies with it. Separate them. Get the strategic position validated first. Then have the capacity conversation.
- **Anti-pattern**: "We should be the hero surface, but we'd need two more engineers to do it right." The second sentence kills the first.
