# Migrate Leadership From a Volume Metric to a Value Metric Without a Cliff

Date identified: 2026-07-24 (n=1 — MCP reporting; watch for a second instance). Source: Pedro session — top management is anchored on MCP "requests" (a ~1.6M raw traffic number), and the true value signal is "specific tool calls" (Value Realization), a far smaller number. The problem: how to move the audience to the value metric without the swap reading as a collapse of the product.

**The trap.** When leadership steers by a big volume number and you replace it with a smaller value number, they do not hear "better instrument." They hear "the product dropped" or "you are minimizing your own work." A direct swap destroys your own headline. This is the audience-facing twin of [[Raw Call Volume Is Mostly the Agent Talking to Itself — Classify Before You Headline]] — that entry is the technical basis (most raw volume is the agent talking to itself); this entry is how you move the *audience* off it.

**The move — change the question, don't correct the number.** Not "1.6M → X" but "how much traffic → how much value." Four mechanics:

1. **Keep the big number, re-label it as reach.** The volume number is not wrong; it is a proof of *reach/plumbing*, not of value. Leaving it on the page as "reach" means nothing is taken away — the audience never feels a loss.
2. **Show the funnel, walk them down.** Reach (raw traffic) → real usage (authenticated/successful, noise stripped) → value (specific value-creating actions). Each step is a *filter that removes noise*, not a decline. The small number arrives as earned, at the bottom of a funnel they watched.
3. **Elevate an existing metric, don't introduce a new one.** If a value metric already appears in your reports as one-of-several (here: "% Value Realization," already one of four agent KPIs), your move is "this one deserves to be THE number," not "here is a new KPI." Far easier to land — they have already seen it.
4. **Give a trajectory, not a level.** Frame the value metric as a *rate* (value actions / total activity) that is small today and that you commit to growing. Volume lets them decide nothing (a request ≠ a value). The value rate tells them *where to invest* (which agent delivers, which does not) — "this is the number you can actually steer by."

**Stage it over two cycles, never one swap.** Cycle 1: headline stays the number they know, and you *plant* the value lens beside it ("reach = portée, Value Realization = impact, and impact is what we'll steer by"). Cycle 2: lead with value, demote volume to a "reach" footnote. Two cycles = they relocated without ever feeling the big number was taken.

**You don't need the final value number to start.** The staging is designed so cycle 1 plants the lens *without* the value figure in hand — you commit to it and land the instrumentation next cycle. Missing the number is not a reason to delay the migration; it is a reason to start with the plant.

**Why it is a Senior Director move.** You may own neither the primitives (MCP servers, agents) nor the pipeline, but the person whose *definition of success* leadership adopts is harder to remove than the person who merely delivers most. Defining the metric the organization steers by is the consistency-layer land-grab applied to measurement.
