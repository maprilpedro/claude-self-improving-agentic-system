# Say the Sentence That Obliges — the Hedge Transfers the Ask

_Section: Operating at Senior Director Level — part of `leadership/`; router = README.md._
- **Date identified**: 2026-08-03 (System Review) · promoted from the memory rule `feedback_first_reply_ownership_sentence`, which carries the operational form. This entry is the generalized PM rule; **it also absorbs the residue of the killed [[H-007]]**.
- **Insight**: **A request only transfers work if the sentence obliges someone.** An imperative, a named owner with a date, or a direct question obliges. A hedge (*"might work — checking"*), a preference (*"we would like"*), or a bare description (*"probably the current process isn't fit for it"*) leaves the reader free to do nothing, so they do nothing — and **the idea goes to whoever states it as a plan.**
- **The test**: *can someone read this and do nothing without being at fault?* If yes, it was information, not a request.
- **The discriminant — and this is the part that makes it fixable.** The variable is not seniority, not preparation, and not time pressure. **People fire cleanly when the authority of the sentence sits in an object outside them** — a missing CODEOWNER, an audit number, a merged PR, a config file. **They hedge when the authority has to be their own** — *I want this, this is my idea, do this for me* — and convert the ask into description, waiting for the other person to draw the conclusion.
- **The evidence, including the positive control**:

  **The 07-17 pair is the controlled experiment**: same person, same thread, same day, same cold conditions, 27 minutes apart. Only the locus of authority differs, and the outcomes are opposite. That is why the variable is authority-locus and not prep-time.

| ❌ Right ask, wrong venue · 2026-06-24, group DM, a PDF attached | *"Adopt two fields on your skills: domain (single home) and when-to-use…"* — sent to the agent owner with his own team's colliding pair cited at 0.44 | **No reply. Ever.** Six weeks later the same owner said in public *"yes, this is a new one I haven't heard before."* |
| ✅ **Same content, published with a number and an ACTION line · 2026-08-06, his forum** | *"Skills Disambiguation Fields audit: 6 conform · 18 warn · 64 fail. **ACTION**: please add the three fields to every skill you own."* | **A JIRA in 48 seconds**, plus two other leads relaying it into their own channels unprompted. |

  **The 08-05 row is the harder confirmation.** It is not a fast rescue inside a live thread — it is a **revival**. The question had already failed once in public, on its merits, with the right people present. What changed on the second attempt was not urgency, seniority or audience but the **arrival of a countable object**: a manifest census (62/63) that made the field's emptiness a fact rather than a worry. **A measurement is the cheapest authority-object available, and it is usually one query away.** ⚠️ Note the shape of the reply he got — *"we can start with"* is a position, not a decision ([[feedback_proposal_vs_decision]]); obliging someone to answer is not the same as obliging them to commit.
- **The venue corollary (added 2026-08-07, from the 06-24 → 08-06 pair).** An obliging sentence sent where nobody else can see it is not an obliging sentence. **The same content, unchanged, ran three deliveries and only one worked:** a DM with a good PDF attached — no reply for six weeks; a thread reply inside the right channel — unseen; a published report carrying a count and a labelled **ACTION** line — acted on in under a minute, and relayed onward by two people who were not asked.
  **So the full form has three parts, not one.** A sentence that obliges · a countable object that makes refusing it a factual claim · **a venue with an audience, so that not acting is visible to someone other than the asker.** Drop the third and the first two are wasted. ⚠️ **The failure is invisible while it is happening** — a DM that gets no reply reads as "sent", and the sender carries it as done until the day someone says in public that they have never heard of it.
  **Corollary for the receipt:** when the ask finally lands, the six weeks are not worth reclaiming. The counterpart has just taken the action; producing the old DM converts a win into a dispute ([[feedback_dont_litigate_prior_replies]]). **Bank the mechanism, not the grievance.**
- **Why it is a Senior Director rule, not a writing tip**: this is [[The Game Itself — Position Over Merit]] in its operational form. Supplying the evidence and waiting for someone to draw the conclusion is the merit game. **A Director supplies the analysis; a Senior Director states what must now happen.** The merit of an observation does not carry an ask — obligation carries it, and authorship follows obligation.
- **How to apply**: scan the *mood* of the sentence, not its content. Kill the insurance words when the claim is yours. Keep a hedge only where the uncertainty is real and load-bearing — and then name who resolves it and by when ([[feedback_lead_with_the_condition]]). When you hold only half the ask, state your half as an obligation and name the half you don't own; splitting honestly still obliges, hedging the whole thing does not.
- **🔬 External validation, same day the rule was promoted (2026-08-03), from an instrument that never saw the internal evidence.** A Workday 360 round returned this from **Felix Meschberger**, a senior architect who works with Pedro weekly: *"I am sometimes not sure, whether he understands, what I am talking about… I have said Pedro is dependable, but **a good scribe is also dependable**."* His stated positive is the same observation inverted — *"very dependable and conscientious in taking notes and recording points of discussion."* **A scribe obliges nobody.** The rule was derived from seven Slack and meeting moments inside one person's own memory; a colleague independently described the identical failure mode from the outside, in one word, without access to any of it. **That is the strongest kind of confirmation a behavioural rule can get, and it also names the cost**: the register that never obliges is eventually read not as politeness but as *not understanding the topic*. Description reads as transcription.
- **The corollary this adds**: an artifact without a visible judgment is filed as notes, regardless of quality. **Every outward artifact needs one sentence where you say what should now happen and would defend it if challenged.** Neutral option-laying, balanced summaries and faithful minutes are all scribe outputs. Polish makes them better minutes, not a position.
- **Distinct from**: [[Bank Your Authorship in Writing When You Contribute Into a Peer's Artifact]] (that is the written trace *after* you contribute; this is the mood of the sentence *at* the moment of the ask).
- **🟢 New instance, 2026-08-10, and it adds a fourth part: pre-concede the cost of your own default.** A decision was owed on which manifest AEM ships into and the room held three positions, two of them Pedro's own within thirty minutes. Rather than post the question, he posted the fork **with a named default and a deadline** — *"We are pointing to cx-coworker manifest, please discuss and raise concern here. **We have to decide by WED 08/12**"* — and under that default he listed its costs himself: *"Higher risk of exposing unfinished skills to customers"*, *"Governance Agent is the sole team on cx-coworker today"*, and that some teams would read the exposure as a plus and some as a concern.
  **Why the self-stated cost is the load-bearing part.** A default with no cost attached reads as a decision already taken and invites people to fight the process instead of the substance. A default whose downside the author has already written down is legible as a *default*: the disagreement has somewhere to go, and the only thing left to argue is whether the cost is acceptable. **He obliged the room without appearing to have closed it** — which is exactly the move that fails when the author hides the weakness and someone else finds it. Pairs with [[Lead the Slide With the Honest Read of Your Own Metric, Not the Flattering Number]]: the honest read is what buys the default its authority.
  **So the full form is now four parts**: a sentence that obliges · a countable object · a venue with an audience · **a named default with a deadline and its cost stated by you.** The fourth is what converts an obliging question into an obliging decision.
