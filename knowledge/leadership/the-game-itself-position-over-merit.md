# The Game Itself — Position Over Merit

> **⭐ Foundational frame. Come back to this one.** Pedro's standing instruction (2026-06-02): keep returning to these rules when framing his work, because his default belief — "good work gets recognized and moves me up" — is false (see `false-beliefs/` FB-031). This section is the unifying frame under most of the rest of this file. Source: Jiang Xueqin wealth-game video, translated from "how dynastic wealth is really built" into "how you actually rise to Senior Director." Keep the diagnosis, drop the video's fatalism (the wealth game is closed; the career game is learnable and open — Pedro can play it).

**The flip.** You've been playing the *merit game*: master your domain, do excellent work, wait for recognition. The people who rise play the *position game*: they own structure, leverage, and the rules. Merit is the story that keeps strong executors grinding at their current level. Doing more good work inside your mastered lane reinforces where you are; it does not change your structural position.

**The five rules** (each maps a wealth-game move onto the career game):

1. **Don't compete — create a monopoly.** *(Video: the rich eliminate competition, they don't compete in it.)* Don't try to be the best in a crowded lane. Be the **only** name on a lane you define and name. → Already lived: AEM–AO liaison (Bertrand-named), the consistency layer, the architecture session you now convene. H-005 (resolved) is the proof this works.
2. **Control what counts as value.** *(Video: the rich decide what becomes money and who can make it.)* Whoever defines the metric, the narrative, the criteria controls the game. You run the reporting — so **own the definition of "value" for AEM agents**, don't just measure it. ("Definition Ownership as a Moat" entry below.)
3. **Write the rules, don't follow them.** *(Video: capture legal privilege, write the laws.)* Be in the room where definitions and decisions get written; become the **author of the framework** others operate inside (surface map, skill-declaration standard). Rule-writer outranks rule-follower at the same skill level.
4. **Proximity to the deciders beats silent excellence.** *(Video: regulatory capture, the revolving door.)* Relationships with the people who decide (Bertrand, Loni, Ian, the VPs) move you more than flawless work nobody senior watches. This is your self-named gap, stated structurally. ("Broadcast Frequency Is the Muscle," "MOC-First Ship.")
5. **Position compounds; effort resets.** *(Video: wealth auto-perpetuates.)* A structural position (being THE name on a domain) generates visibility and opportunity on its own, every cycle. Pure effort has to be re-spent each review period and is forgotten. Invest in position, not in another round of output.

**The one-line test for any week's work:** *Is this building my position (a lane only I hold / a definition I own / a room I'm in / a relationship with a decider), or is it just more excellent output inside my current box?* If it's the latter, it may be necessary, but it is not moving you up — name it as such and don't mistake it for progress toward Senior Director.
