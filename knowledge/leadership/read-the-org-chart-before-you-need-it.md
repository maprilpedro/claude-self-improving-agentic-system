# Read the Org Chart Before You Need It

_Section: Cross-Org Influence Without Authority — part of `leadership/`; router = README.md._
- **Date identified**: 2026-04-16
- **Source**: Pedro spent ~30 minutes browsing the Adobe org chart (21 screenshots captured). The exercise revealed multiple structural facts that materially changed his strategic reads: Silvia Mulet Ferre manages Eugene Bannykh (she's Adobe Design, not AEP); Jean-Michel Pittet is VP of AEM Engineering (peer VP to Loni, not "role TBD"); Felix Delval is Senior Engineering Manager (not IC); Angela Han is in Shivakumar's Engineering chain, not AEP PM; Haresh Kumar is Sr Director Product Marketing (not Field/Sales PM).
- **Pattern**: Director-level work happens inside a single org. Senior Director work happens across orgs. You cannot operate across orgs without a map of who reports to whom, where VP peers sit, which chains your stakeholders trace up to. Most people read the org chart only when a specific need forces them to (a new meeting, a new ask). Senior Directors read the org chart proactively, before they need it — because it reveals structural facts that change how they frame every subsequent interaction.
- **What the exercise revealed that directly changed Pedro's strategic reads**:
  1. **Cross-org relationships he misread.** Silvia was listed as "UX Manager reporting to Loni" — wrong. She's Adobe Design, and she manages Eugene. That reframed the entire Rubin thread from "AEP power play" to "cross-product design collaboration."
  2. **Upcoming high-stakes meeting revalued.** Jean-Michel was "role TBD" in notes. Actually he's VP Engineering AEM. The Loni + Jean-Michel meeting is the top AEM leadership pair, not a generic Loni update. Preparation effort should match.
  3. **Peer weight recalibrated.** Felix (Sr Eng Manager, reports to VP Michael Marth two hops up), Conrad (Sr Principal Architect, direct to Jean-Michel), Ian Boston (Principal Scientist direct to VP Alexander Saar) all carry more structural weight than generic titles suggest. Bringing them to cross-org meetings changes the optics.
  4. **Collaborative tone decoded.** Angela Han was friendly not because Pedro charmed her, but because she's in Engineering and not competing on product scope. Understanding the *why* behind a tone stops you from misreading it.
- **How to apply**: Schedule ~30 minutes once per quarter (or when entering a new scope) to walk the org chart. Capture chains for: your direct chain, your peer VPs' chains, the VP chains of adjacent orgs you touch, and the chains of anyone who has contacted you in the last month. Save as stakeholder map entries or a dedicated reporting-chains artifact. The insight compounds — each interaction you have afterward gets contextualized by structure.
- **Senior Director signal**: The willingness to invest time in structural intel, not just task execution, is a Senior Director operating habit. Directors execute within the structure. Senior Directors understand the structure so they can operate across it.
