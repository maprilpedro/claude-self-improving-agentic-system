# The Director→Senior Director Gap Is a Communication/Visibility Gap, Not a Capability Gap

_Section: Operating at Senior Director Level — part of `leadership/`; router = README.md._

- **Date promoted**: 2026-05-27 (proposed 2026-03-19 as H-003; confirmed and promoted from `hypotheses/`).
- **Source**: H-003 confirmed over 2026-03 → 2026-05. Origins: Gallo *Presentation Secrets* + Mehrabian; Pedro's self-named visibility gap.
- **The rule**: for an execution-strong Director, the *marginal* bar to Senior Director is overwhelmingly visibility / communication / positioning — not capability or output. Leadership perception is driven far more by how ideas are framed, how often they are broadcast, and who is in the room than by what is delivered.
- **Confirming evidence (3+, 0 against)**:
  - Behavioral self-diagnosis (2026-04-02): execution bias + validation-seeking — both positioning problems, not capability.
  - Broadcast-frequency reps (May 1-8): the gap moved on frequency of broadcasting, not better work.
  - Ian NorthStar thread (2026-05): a well-placed *question* (not a deliverable) moved the architect to ship the NorthStar next day and escalate memory ownership to Saar (VP). Influence via communication.
  - Cross-confirmed by resolved [[H-005]]: cross-org influence accrued from owning and *naming* the substrate before any standard shipped.
- **Operating implications**: subtract-don't-add when overwhelmed (calibration is a Senior Director move); [[Broadcast Frequency Is the Muscle, Not Strategic Comms|broadcast frequency is the muscle]] (reps, not frameworks); a well-aimed question or public naming at altitude can beat another artifact; track reactive-ownership reps separately from proactive ones (H-007).
- **Boundary**: capability is necessary — you cannot communicate past bad work. The rule says the *marginal* gap, given strong work, is communication. Don't read it as "output doesn't matter."
