## Decision: announce EPA on 2026-08-24 relying on the informal AI Ethics non-blocking, and carry it

**Date:** 2026-08-12. **Decision owner: Pedro Ferreira.** Asked for a call between two options, made it.

## Context

AI Ethics on EPA still reads *"team will evaluate end of Aug"*. That is after the 08-14 sign-off date and after the 08-24 GA. It is the only sign-off whose own date falls outside the calendar, and it sits on **Corey Dulimba's agent, not Pedro's**.

Under the decision of 2026-08-07 the readiness checklist is the **announcement bar, not a release gate**. A red row does not stop a team writing `ga`. So AI Ethics cannot block the release. **It can only block the announcement, which is the half Pedro owns.** The risk therefore lands entirely on him.

This was one of the **two slots left deliberately blank** in the 2026-08-10 heads-up to Bertrand (DM `DQ6H0AV7H`, ts `1786361855.054289`), because only Pedro could fill them. It stayed blank for two days. The other blank, cover for the 08-10 → 08-21 escalation window, is still open.

## The decision

**Announce the portfolio on 08-24 with EPA included**, on the basis of the informal non-blocking, and own the exposure.

**And do not name it in the note to Loni** (same day, Pedro's call). Writing an unresolved sign-off into a VP note gives that VP a reason to ask AI Ethics directly, and a question arriving from that level converts an informal non-block into a formal review with a formal answer. The mention was removed from the draft.

## Alternative rejected

**Announce the portfolio without EPA and add it once the sign-off lands.** Rejected: EPA is the agent with the customers and the demos, it is in Corey and Brian's Developers Live session on 09-01, and a portfolio announcement missing its most-used agent reads as a programme that is not ready.

## What has to be true for this to hold

1. 🔴 **Verify a named AI Ethics non-block actually exists for EPA.** What is on record is a preliminary non-blocking from **Legal, ORR and Security**. AI Ethics is a separate body and no named person is recorded saying it is not blocking. **If that non-block does not exist, this decision rests on nothing.** Route: Yanira, who is running the sign-offs.
2. **Get it in writing with a name and a date before 08-24.** "Informal" with a named person and a date is a defensible basis. "Informal" with nobody attached is not.
3. **Tell Corey the basis.** He owns EPA. Announcing his agent on an informal non-block without telling him makes it Pedro's alone and makes it a surprise if it turns. Telling him makes it a shared, recorded position.

## The exposure Pedro accepted

If AI Ethics returns a finding on EPA after 08-24, Adobe has already said publicly that EPA is generally available. That is a retraction on someone else's agent, in a public forum, one week before Developers Live.
